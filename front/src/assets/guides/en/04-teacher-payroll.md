# Teacher payroll

Menu **Financial Management › Teacher payroll**, for the administrator only.

The teacher receives **a share of what their group's series has actually collected**, at the percentage chosen when paying. A student who does not pay lowers the payout; so does a refund.

Three tabs: **To pay**, **Payouts**, **Rates**.

## Creating the rates

**Rates** tab: a label and the teacher's share, for example "Standard", 60 %. The school's share, 40 %, is calculated.

> [!NOTE]
> Editing or disabling a rate changes no payout already made: each payout keeps its own copy.

## The To pay tab

| State | What it means |
|---|---|
| **To pay** | all sessions are validated: **Pay** button |
| **In progress** | sessions remain to be validated (the number is shown) |
| **No teacher** | attach a teacher to the group first |
| **Nothing collected** | no net collection: nothing to share |
| **To adjust** | already paid, but money came in or was refunded since |
| **Paid** | up to date; visible when **Show paid series** is ticked |

The sessions column shows "validated / created", and the **planned** number when it differs: "0 / 2 (3 planned)".

## Paying a series

**Pay** → choose the rate → **Calculate** → read the calculation → **Record the payout** → **Print the slip** → have both copies signed.

> [!TIP]
> 74,000 DA paid − 2,000 DA refunded = 72,000 DA net. 72,000 × 60 % = **43,200 DA** for the teacher, 28,800 DA for the school.

> [!WARNING]
> "Incomplete series": a planned session has not been created yet. The payout is still possible; money for a session added later will go through an adjustment.

<!-- page -->

## Money received or refunded after the payout

The series goes back to **To adjust**. **Adjust** calculates the difference at the percentage of the original payout:

- **top-up** if money came in: +3,000 DA gives the teacher 1,800 DA more;
- **deduction** if money was refunded: −1,000 DA, the teacher gives back 600 DA.

Nothing is automatic: you make the adjustment.

> [!NOTE]
> If an amount changes between the calculation and the recording, the new calculation is shown with a warning: read it again, then confirm.

## Correcting a payout

**Payouts** tab:

- **Cancel the payout**: reason, preview, confirmation. The payout stays visible, marked "Cancelled", and the series is **To pay** again.
- **Redo at another rate**: cancels the payout and redoes it for the **same teacher**. To pay another teacher: cancel, then pay again.

Only the **most recent** payout of a series can be corrected; otherwise the screen names the one to correct first.

To reprint, use the printer button. From the second print on, the slip is marked **DUPLICATE no.…**; a cancelled payout is marked **CANCELLED**.

<!-- page -->

## While a series is paid

You cannot unvalidate, delete or deactivate a session of the series, nor move it to another group. The message says: "cancel this payout before…".

Cancel the payout first, most recent first, make the change, then pay again.

## Good to know

- A series from a **closed year** can still be paid, as long as it is to pay.
- A **catch-up** collected in a host group counts in that group's collection.
- **Substitute teachers** are not handled: the group's teacher receives the whole share.
