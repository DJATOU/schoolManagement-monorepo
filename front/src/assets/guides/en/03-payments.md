# Payments

## Where to collect a payment

Payments are collected **from the student's profile**:

1. Top bar: **Students** tab, type the name, open the profile.
2. Click **Ajouter un paiement** (this button keeps its French label).

The **Financial Management › Payments** menu is for searching and checking payments (filters, CSV export, history) and for refunds. Payments are not collected there.

> [!NOTE]
> On a past year, or for a Viewer account, the payment button is greyed out.

## The "Add a payment" window

1. Choose the **Group**, then the **Session series**. A settled series is greyed out.
2. Read the summary: "Series: 4 session(s) × 800 DA = 3,200 DA", then what is already paid and what remains.
3. Enter the **Amount paid**, or tick **Pay the whole series**.
4. Choose the **Payment method**: Cash, Cheque, Bank card or Other.
5. Click **Confirm**, read the summary again, then confirm.

The receipt **RECU-YYYY-NNNN** is sent to the printer.

## What the series costs

**Series cost = billable sessions × price of one session**, minus the student's discount if there is one.

A student who joins during a series **does not pay for past sessions** they did not attend. The window says so: "2 session(s) not billed to this student", with the reason "Session held before enrolment".

> [!TIP]
> A series of 4 sessions at 800 DA. Inès enrols just before the 3rd session: she pays 2 × 800 = **1,600 DA**, not 3,200 DA.

## Up to date or overdue

The status compares what the student has paid with what they have **already attended**:

- **due so far = attended sessions × price of one session**;
- **Overdue** if the amount paid is lower than what is due so far, otherwise **Up to date**.

A student can therefore pay session by session and stay up to date. An absence never raises the amount due so far.

> [!TIP]
> 800 DA per session. Lucas attended 2 sessions (1,600 DA) and paid 1,600 DA: he is **Up to date**, even though the series costs 3,200 DA. After his 3rd attended session, he is **Overdue** until he has paid 2,400 DA.

## Carrying over the surplus

If the amount exceeds what the chosen series owes, the surplus moves **by itself** to the group's next series. The window shows it before you confirm, under **Payment split**: "Applied to …", "Carried over to …".

> [!TIP]
> 1,600 DA remain due on the October series and the student pays 3,200 DA: 1,600 DA settle October, 1,600 DA are carried over to the next series.

> [!WARNING]
> If no following series has sessions, the payment is **refused in full**, and the maximum that can be collected is shown. Create the sessions of the next series first, or bring the amount down to that maximum.

## Receipts

Every payment has its receipt: amount received, student, group, series, payment method, **series situation** (cost, billed sessions, total paid, remaining) and how the payment was split.

To reprint it: student profile, **Payments received** block, **Reprint receipt** button.

A cancelled receipt is stamped **CANCELLED** and, if it was replaced, shows the number of the new receipt.

> [!NOTE]
> A receipt only covers the payment it mentions: it is not a discharge for the whole series.

## Correcting a payment

Student profile, **Payments received** block:

- **Cancel**: choose the reason, **See preview**, then **Confirm**. The receipt is cancelled.
- **Correct**: changing the amount, student, group or series creates a **new receipt** that replaces the old one. Changing only the payment method or the note keeps the same receipt.

The preview shows, before and after: series cost, due so far, paid, remaining and status.

> [!NOTE]
> A payment that has already been refunded cannot be corrected: the screen names the refund involved.

## Refunds

**Payments** menu: on the payment's line, the **Record a refund** button.

1. Read the **Amount paid**, **Already refunded** and **Refundable cap**.
2. Enter the **Amount to refund** and the **Refund reason**. The reason is required: it is printed on the receipt.
3. Click **Refund**, then **Confirm and refund**.

The refund receipt **REMB-YYYY-NNNN** is printed; a reprint is marked **DUPLICATE no.…**.

> [!WARNING]
> A refund cannot be cancelled in the application.

## Discounts

Menu **Financial Management › Discounts**, then **Add a discount**: the student, the scope (**Group**, **Series** or **Session**) and the rate. At 100 % it is an **Exemption**.

The discount lowers the price of a session, and the payment window says so:

> "20 % discount: 640 DA per session instead of 800 DA."

**Edit rate** or **Delete discount** recalculates the amounts due.

## The correction log

On the student's profile, the **Correction log** lists every correction: the date, what changed, the effect on the amount due, the reason and who made it. It covers payments, enrolments, attendance, justifications and catch-ups.

Pick the dates (**From**, **To**, **Show**), then **Print** to answer a family who disputes an amount.

> [!NOTE]
> Nothing is corrected without a reason: that is what makes everything explainable later.
